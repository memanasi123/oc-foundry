Privacy Policy

Your privacy,
respected.

Last updated: 20 August 2026. OC Foundry is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we use it.

What we collect

Character data: When you generate a character, it is stored temporarily in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to purchase the character bible PDF.

Payment information: When you make a purchase, payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store or have access to your card details. Razorpay collects the information necessary to process the transaction and may collect additional data as described in their Privacy Policy..

Email address (optional): If you choose to have your PDF emailed to you, we collect your email address only to send that one email. We do not add you to a mailing list, share your email, or contact you again unless required by law or your specific request.

Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymized data such as pages visited, time on site, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Advertising: Our site may display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for site functionality. Third-party services (Razorpay for payments, Google AdSense for advertising) may set their own cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we use your data

  • To provide the character generator service
  • To process payments securely
  • To deliver purchased character bibles by email (if requested)
  • To improve the site experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share data with:

  • Razorpay: for payment processing
  • Resend: for email delivery (if you request email delivery)
  • Vercel: our hosting provider for the backend
  • GitHub Pages: our hosting provider for the website

Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for any data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via the contact page.

Data retention

Payment records are kept as long as required by law (typically 6-7 years for tax purposes). Email addresses used for one-time PDF delivery are deleted within 30 days. Character data in your browser is only kept until you clear your browser storage.

Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via our contact page.
